Solo mode

Solo can be used without the multiplayer backend. Gameplay progress, settings and functional statistics are stored locally on the device.

Online Challenge

Online Challenge requires internet access. On first Online use, a player chooses a nickname and accepts the Online Terms and privacy notice. The backend creates an opaque guest identifier and short-lived, revocable session credentials. It processes presence, challenges/invitations and match state/results needed to operate multiplayer.

The edge provider derives a two-letter country code from network connection metadata to display an approximate country flag. It may be inaccurate. The app does not request GPS/precise-location permission. Application code does not expose a raw IP address to other players; the infrastructure provider necessarily processes network metadata to provide HTTPS/WSS and abuse protection.

We do not ask for email, phone number, real name, password, birth date, contacts, social login, profile picture or precise location. There is no public chat, private messaging or user image/audio/file upload in this release.

Blocks, reports and retention

Players can block or report visible nicknames using limited reason categories. Presence/join requests expire quickly. The source defaults are 1 hour for post-match object state, 90 days for moderation evidence and 31 days for identity-deletion tombstones; the publisher must review these before deployment.

Delete/reset Online identity

Settings includes Delete/Reset Online Identity. After an authenticated delete succeeds, local Online identity/session state is removed. Limited moderation evidence may remain for its disclosed retention period where needed for safety, abuse prevention or legal compliance.

Advertising and consent

Ads are disabled by default. If the publisher enables Google AdMob, consent information must be refreshed at app launch through Google UMP, ads must only be requested when permitted, and a privacy-options entry point must be shown when required. Online gameplay does not depend on advertising consent.

Security and transport

Production Online traffic uses HTTPS/WSS. Backend signing/session secrets are server-side only and are not shipped in the app.

Contact

For any question about this policy, or to request deletion of an Online identity, write to quizzy.quest.planet@gmail.com.

Advertising, the advertising ID, and your choices

لَمَّةُ الْمَعْرِفَةِ (Lammat Al-Ma'rifa) (com.novanest.maani) uses Google AdMob for advertising. This section is the disclosure Google requires of every publisher that uses its advertising SDKs, and it matches what is declared in the Play Console Data safety form for this app.

What is collected, and by whom

We do not collect, store or transmit personal data ourselves. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app does collect data in order to serve and measure ads. It reads the Android advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned by your device — and sends it with each ad request, together with coarse technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, and the IP address that every network request necessarily carries.

That identifier is used to select ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance, and detect fraudulent activity. It is sent to Google, which may make it available to its advertising partners. We receive only aggregate reporting — how many ads were shown and what they earned. Those reports contain nothing that identifies any individual.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your controls

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID. On most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving it at all.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Where the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive apply, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform, a Consent Management Platform certified by Google, before any personalised ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy options entry in the app's settings.
  • Non-personalised ads. If you decline consent, ads may still be shown but are not personalised using your advertising ID.
