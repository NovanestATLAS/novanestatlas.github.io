What it is

A focused practice app for people preparing for the ECL (English Comprehension Level) and ALCPT (American Language Course Placement Test). It combines explained multiple-choice questions, US English text-to-speech, review of your own mistakes, and exam-style timed tests.

What's inside

Practice

  • 1,900+ multiple-choice questions, each with an explanation
  • 306 gap-fill drill items across 15 drill sets
  • Module tests and exam-style timed tests

Reference library

  • 115 American idioms with meanings and examples
  • 112 irregular verbs with audio
  • Search across questions, idioms and verbs

Topics covered

  • Tenses, modals, passive, conditionals, reported speech
  • Prepositions, determiners, sentence structure
  • Vocabulary in context and American usage
  • American idioms and phrasal verbs
  • Military and defence vocabulary

Hands-free study

The app reads the question and all four answer choices aloud in US English, with a real pause where the blank sits. After you answer, it can read the correct choice and the explanation as well. A full practice session works with the screen off — while driving, walking, or during downtime.

Smart review

Questions you get wrong go into a mistake box and come back on a spaced schedule until you answer them correctly and consistently. The home screen highlights the study area where your current average is lowest.

Privacy and cost

The app works offline. No account, no registration, and core learning content is stored on your device. It is free and supported by ads shown between sections — never during a timed test. There is no paid version and nothing is locked. Read the full privacy policy.
