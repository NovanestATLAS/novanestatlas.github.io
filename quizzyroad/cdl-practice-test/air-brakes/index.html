        Sample questions

        A few questions from the set. Try to answer before opening each explanation.

        1. If you always drive a straight truck or combination with working ABS on all axles, what may you do in an emergency stop?

          • A. Use only the parking brake
          • B. Fully apply the brakes
          • C. Pump the brake pedal as fast as you can
          • D. Shift into neutral before braking
          Show answer

          Answer: B. The one exception to braking as you always have is a vehicle with working ABS on every axle, where full brake application is allowed in an emergency stop.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 5.4.2 Braking with Antilock Brakes.

        2. During the engine compartment checks, what should you check on a belt-driven air compressor?

          • A. The cut-out pressure shown on the dash gauges
          • B. The thickness of the brake linings
          • C. The color of the compressor's air filter
          • D. The condition and tightness of the compressor drive belt
          Show answer

          Answer: D. When the compressor is driven by a belt, the engine compartment check includes making sure that belt is in good shape and properly tight.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 5.3.1 During Step 2 Engine Compartment Checks.

        3. During the final air brake check, how do you lower tank pressure to see whether the compressor cuts in?

          • A. Run the engine at a fast idle
          • B. With the engine idling, step on and off the brake
          • C. Rev the engine several times in neutral
          • D. Wait with the engine idling and the brakes off
          Show answer

          Answer: B. Pressing and releasing the brake pedal uses up air, so the gauge falls until the governor should let the compressor start pumping again.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 5.3.3 Step 7 Final Air Brake Check.

        4. What can happen if you press and release the brake pedal when there is no need to?

          • A. Air can be let out faster than the compressor can replace it
          • B. The governor raises the cut-out pressure
          • C. The spring brakes release by themselves
          • D. The brake linings cool down faster
          Show answer

          Answer: A. Every release of the pedal lets some compressed air escape, so needless pumping can drain the tanks faster than the compressor refills them, and the brakes stop working if pressure gets too low.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 5.1.7 The Brake Pedal.

        5. In stab braking, after you release the brakes, how long can it take for the wheels to start rolling?

          • A. Up to five seconds
          • B. Up to ten seconds
          • C. Up to one second
          • D. Up to thirty seconds
          Show answer

          Answer: C. The wheels may need as long as a second to roll again, and reapplying the brakes before they do will keep the vehicle from straightening out.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 5.4.3 Emergency Stops.

        Frequently asked questions

        What score do I need to pass?

        Federal rules require at least 80% correct on each CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135 (eCFR)).

        Are these the questions on the real test?

        No. Real test questions are not public. We write our own questions from the official manual, so they cover the same material but are not copies of test questions.

        Is the CDL manual the same in every state?

        State CDL manuals are built on the same national model manual from AAMVA, so the knowledge content is largely the same. We compared 3 state editions for this test: 45 of 45 quotes appear word for word in the same section of all of them. Your own state’s manual is the final word if anything differs.

        How do you check the answers?

        Each question stores the manual section (or page) and an exact quote that a script checks against the manual text, then an independent AI review answers it from that part of the manual alone. See our editorial policy. Found a mistake? Tell us.
