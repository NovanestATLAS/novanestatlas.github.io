        Sample questions

        A few questions from the set. Try to answer before opening each explanation.

        1. Where must riders not be allowed to leave their carry-on baggage?

          • A. In a doorway or aisle
          • B. Under their own seats
          • C. On the overhead racks
          • D. In the baggage bay below
          Show answer

          Answer: A. Bags left in a doorway or aisle can trip other riders, so those spaces must stay clear of carry-ons.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 4.2 Loading and Trip Start.

        2. What type of tires are not allowed on the front wheels of a bus?

          • A. Tires with a deep mud and snow tread
          • B. Tubeless tires with steel belts
          • C. Tires with a higher load rating
          • D. Recapped or re-grooved tires
          Show answer

          Answer: D. Part of the tire check before driving is making sure neither front wheel has a tire that was recapped or re-grooved.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 4.1.1 Vehicle Systems.

        3. Where do bus accidents often happen?

          • A. On long, straight highways
          • B. In bus parking garages
          • C. On divided four-lane roads
          • D. At intersections
          Show answer

          Answer: D. Intersections are a common place for bus crashes, so stay careful there even when signals or stop signs control the other traffic.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 4.3.3 Common Accidents.

        4. At which railroad crossing do you not have to stop, though you must still slow down and check for other vehicles?

          • A. One on a rural road without gates
          • B. One where the tracks look clear
          • C. One where the crossing lights are off
          • D. One where a flagman is directing traffic
          Show answer

          Answer: D. A stop is not required where a police officer or flagman is directing traffic, but you must still slow down and look carefully for other vehicles.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 4.3.5 Railroad-highway Crossing/ Stops.

        5. No rider may stand forward of:

          • A. The rear of the driver's seat
          • B. The front edge of the stairwell
          • C. The first row of rider seats
          • D. The center of the front door
          Show answer

          Answer: A. Standing riders must stay behind the back of the driver's seat, and buses that allow standing mark this limit on the floor.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 4.2.3 Standee Line.

        Frequently asked questions

        What score do I need to pass?

        Federal rules require at least 80% correct on each CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135 (eCFR)).

        Are these the questions on the real test?

        No. Real test questions are not public. We write our own questions from the official manual, so they cover the same material but are not copies of test questions.

        Is the CDL manual the same in every state?

        State CDL manuals are built on the same national model manual from AAMVA, so the knowledge content is largely the same. We compared 3 state editions for this test: 30 of 30 quotes appear word for word in the same section of all of them. Your own state’s manual is the final word if anything differs.

        How do you check the answers?

        Each question stores the manual section (or page) and an exact quote that a script checks against the manual text, then an independent AI review answers it from that part of the manual alone. See our editorial policy. Found a mistake? Tell us.
