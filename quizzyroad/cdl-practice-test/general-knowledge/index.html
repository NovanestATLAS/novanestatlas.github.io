        Sample questions

        A few questions from the set. Try to answer before opening each explanation.

        1. Sample question 1

          Your ABS stops working. What does that mean for braking?

          • AYou still have regular brakes; drive normally and get it serviced soon
          • BYou have no brakes and must stop at once
          • CYou must brake only with the parking brake
          • DYou must pump the brakes at all times
          Show answer

          Answer: A. ABS is added to the normal brakes. If it fails, the normal brakes still work, but have the system repaired soon.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 2.18.7 Braking If ABS Is Not Working.

        2. Sample question 2

          What is controlled braking?

          • ABraking fully and releasing when the wheels lock
          • BBraking as hard as you can without locking the wheels
          • CPumping the pedal as fast as possible
          • DUsing only the parking brake
          Show answer

          Answer: B. Controlled braking keeps the wheels rolling while you brake hard, with only small steering moves.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 2.17.2 How to Stop Quickly and Safely.

        3. Sample question 3

          Compared with hand-held phones, how likely are hands-free devices to distract you?

          • AThey never distract you
          • BThey distract you only in heavy traffic
          • CThey are half as likely to distract you
          • DThey are no less likely to distract you
          Show answer

          Answer: D. Your attention leaves the road with either kind of phone, so hands-free does not remove the distraction.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 2.9.4 Cell/Mobile Phones.

        4. Sample question 4

          In winter, you notice there is no spray coming off other vehicles' tires. What does this tell you?

          • AThe road is dry and safe
          • BIce has formed on the road
          • COther drivers have chains on
          • DThe temperature is rising
          Show answer

          Answer: B. When tires stop throwing up spray on a winter road, the water has likely frozen.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 2.13.2 Driving.

        5. Sample question 5

          During an inspection you see rust around the wheel nuts. What might this mean?

          • AThe nuts may be loose
          • BThe tire is underinflated
          • CThe brakes are overheating
          • DThe wheel bearing needs oil
          Show answer

          Answer: A. Rust streaks around wheel nuts can be a sign of looseness, so check how tight they are.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 2.1.3 What to Look For.

        Frequently asked questions

        What score do I need to pass?

        Federal rules require at least 80% correct on each CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135 (eCFR)).

        Are these the questions on the real test?

        No. Real test questions are not public. We write our own questions from the official manual, so they cover the same material but are not copies of test questions.

        Is the CDL manual the same in every state?

        State CDL manuals are built on the same national model manual from AAMVA, so the knowledge content is largely the same. We compared 3 state editions for this test: 112 of 113 quotes appear word for word in the same section of all of them. Your own state’s manual is the final word if anything differs.

        How do you check the answers?

        Each question stores the manual section (or page) and an exact quote that a script checks against the manual text, then an independent AI review answers it from that part of the manual alone. See our editorial policy. Found a mistake? Tell us.
