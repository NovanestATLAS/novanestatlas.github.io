        Sample questions

        A few questions from the set. Try to answer before opening each explanation.

        1. When may you accept the shipper's certification that a package is properly packaged?

          • A. Unless a package is clearly unsafe or not HMR compliant
          • B. Only after you open and inspect each package yourself
          • C. Only when the shipper is also your own employer
          • D. Never, because the driver must re-certify every package
          Show answer

          Answer: A. You can rely on the shipper's signed certification about packaging unless a package is obviously unsafe or out of line with the regulations.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 9.3.6 Shipper's Certification.

        2. You are using DANGEROUS placards for a mixed Table 2 load. You must use the specific placard for a Table 2 material if you loaded how much of it at one place?

          • A. 1,001 pounds or more
          • B. 2,205 pounds or more
          • C. 3,000 pounds or more
          • D. 5,000 pounds or more
          Show answer

          Answer: B. The DANGEROUS placard cannot stand in for the specific placard of any Table 2 material when 2,205 pounds or more of it was loaded at a single place.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 9.3.11 Placard Tables.

        3. While a vehicle containing hazardous materials is being fueled, where must someone always be?

          • A. In the cab, keeping the engine running
          • B. At the rear of the trailer, watching traffic
          • C. Inside the station, paying for the fuel
          • D. At the nozzle, controlling the fuel flow
          Show answer

          Answer: D. With the engine shut off, a person has to stay at the fuel nozzle the whole time and control the flow.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 9.6.7 Refuel with Engine Off.

        4. Your vehicle with an empty tank used for Class 3 flammable liquids breaks down. Which stopped vehicle signals should you use?

          • A. Reflective triangles or red electric lights
          • B. Flares, because the tank is now empty
          • C. Fuses placed on the road behind the vehicle
          • D. Any burning signal set at least 100 feet back
          Show answer

          Answer: A. Burning signals are not allowed around flammable liquid tanks even when they are empty, so reflective triangles or red electric lights are used.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 9.6.4 No Flares!.

        5. Whom are the hazardous materials regulations intended to protect?

          • A. Only the shipper and the carrier company
          • B. You, others nearby, and the environment
          • C. Only police and firefighters at a crash
          • D. Only the owner of the cargo being moved
          Show answer

          Answer: B. The rules exist to keep the driver, nearby people and the environment safe, not just one business or group.

          Source: Commercial Driver's License Manual, Version 07/20/2026 (form DMV 60A), section 9.1.1 Contain the Material.

        Frequently asked questions

        What score do I need to pass?

        Federal rules require at least 80% correct on each CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135 (eCFR)).

        Are these the questions on the real test?

        No. Real test questions are not public. We write our own questions from the official manual, so they cover the same material but are not copies of test questions.

        Is the CDL manual the same in every state?

        State CDL manuals are built on the same national model manual from AAMVA, so the knowledge content is largely the same. We compared 3 state editions for this test: 69 of 69 quotes appear word for word in the same section of all of them. Your own state’s manual is the final word if anything differs.

        How do you check the answers?

        Each question stores the manual section (or page) and an exact quote that a script checks against the manual text, then an independent AI review answers it from that part of the manual alone. See our editorial policy. Found a mistake? Tell us.
