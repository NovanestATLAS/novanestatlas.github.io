Accessibility statement

We want everyone preparing for a permit or CDL test to be able to use QuizzyRoad. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What we do

  • Answers are real buttons. You can answer with the number keys 1-4 or the letter keys A-D.
  • Every interactive element shows a visible focus outline.
  • Correct and incorrect feedback is announced to screen readers and never relies on color alone.
  • Text and controls meet AA color contrast in both light and dark themes.
  • Pages work at 200% zoom and on small screens, and respect the reduced-motion setting.
  • Questions, explanations and guides are plain text, not images.

Known limitations

Ads, when shown, are provided by Google and we cannot fully control their accessibility. They are kept away from quiz controls.

Feedback

If something is hard to use, email quizzy.quest.planet@gmail.com with the page address and what happened. We will reply and fix it.

QuizzyRoad is an independent study site and is not affiliated with any DMV, state agency or the FMCSA.
