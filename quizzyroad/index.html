How our practice questions are made

Real exam questions are not public, so we write our own. Every question starts from a specific section of an official manual published by a state licensing agency or another official issuer. We store the manual, the section (and page when the manual has one) and a short exact quote with each question, and a script checks that the quote really appears there before anything is published.

Drafts are written with AI assistance, then checked automatically and by an independent AI review, and questions that are flagged or sampled are reviewed by a person. Read the full process in our editorial policy.

What the practice tests are, and what they are not

They are a study tool to help you learn what the manual says and to find the topics you still need to review. They are not the official test and we cannot promise any result. The official manual for your state is always the final word, so read it and check anything that matters with your licensing agency.

For example, federal rules require a score of at least 80% on each CDL knowledge test (49 CFR 383.135 (eCFR)). Other details, such as the number of questions, can vary by state, so we only show them when we can link the official source.

Work in progress. The CDL knowledge tests are ready. Driver’s permit tests are being prepared state by state, and each one goes live only after every question on it passes our checks.
