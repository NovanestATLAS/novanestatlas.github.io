Reporting a question error

Error reports are the most useful messages we get. To help us fix it quickly, please include:

  • the test name (for example “CDL Air Brakes”) and your state,
  • the question text, or the page address and question number,
  • what you think the right answer is, and the part of the manual that says so, if you have it.

Other questions

We cannot help with appointments, fees, documents or test results. Please contact your state’s licensing agency for those. We are an independent study site and are not part of any DMV or government agency.
