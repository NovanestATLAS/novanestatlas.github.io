The short version

The app collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own. Every calculation and every saved job stays on your phone. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK sends, and you choose whether that is personalised. Everything below is detail on those two facts.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves the following in its own storage on your device:

  • Your saved jobs and every result you added to them
  • Your settings: theme, default units, rounding precision and waste factor
  • Your favourite and recently used tools
  • Counters used to space out advertisements
  • Whether the unlimited-jobs unlock has been earned

All of it stays on your device. None of it is uploaded, backed up to a server, synchronised across devices, or seen by us. We cannot read it and we have no copy of it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. There is no other copy anywhere.

The app contains no account system, no sign-up, no login and no email collection. We do not know who you are.

The app itself makes no network requests

Every calculator, formula and unit table is packaged inside the app at install time. The app does not download content, does not check for updates by itself, and does not send anything you do to any server. Cleartext traffic is disabled at the manifest level. In airplane mode every calculator works exactly the same. The only network traffic comes from the advertising component described below.

Advertising, the advertising ID, and your choices

Job Site Calc (com.novanest.jobsitecalc) shows ads through Google AdMob. This section is the disclosure Google requires of every publisher that uses its advertising SDKs.

What is collected, and by whom

We do not collect, store or transmit personal data ourselves. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app does collect data in order to serve and measure ads. It reads the Android advertising ID, a resettable identifier assigned by your device, and sends it with each ad request together with coarse technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, and the IP address every network request necessarily carries.

That identifier is used for selecting ads, limiting how often you see the same one, measuring performance, and detecting fraudulent activity. It is sent to Google, which may make it available to its advertising partners. We receive only aggregate reporting — how many ads were shown and what they earned. Those reports contain nothing that identifies any individual.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your controls

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID. On most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving it at all.
  • Consent, in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Where the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive apply, the app requests consent through Google's User Messaging Platform, a Consent Management Platform certified by Google, before any personalised ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app.
  • Non-personalised ads. If you decline consent, ads may still be shown but are not personalised using your advertising ID, and every ad request is flagged accordingly.

Rewarded ads

Three saved jobs are free. Watching one optional rewarded ad unlocks unlimited saved jobs permanently. The unlock is recorded on your device only. Nothing is purchased, no payment details are involved, and the app contains no in-app purchases.

Children

This app is a professional tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Ad content is rated for a general or teen audience as declared in the Play Console.

Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on any server, so there is nothing on our side to retain or delete. Data handled by Google is retained under Google's own policies, linked above. Everything the app stores stays on your device and is removed by deleting your saved jobs or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions the app does not request

The app does not ask for, and cannot access:

LocationNot requested. No GPS, no coarse location.
CameraNot requested.
MicrophoneNot requested. There is no recording and no speech recognition.
ContactsNot requested.
Files, photos and mediaNot requested.
Call logs, SMS, phoneNot requested.
CalendarNot requested.

The app declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, which Google requires of any app that uses the advertising ID on Android 13 and above. That single declaration is the whole of what the app asks for.

Exporting a job sheet uses your device's own share sheet. The app hands the text to Android and Android decides where it goes. No storage or contacts permission is involved and the app never sees which app you chose.

No analytics, no crash reporting

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporting SDK and no attribution SDK. Nothing counts your taps, times your sessions or reports what you calculated.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the GDPR, gives you rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability over personal data held about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. You already hold everything the app produces, on your own device, and you can erase it at any moment without asking anyone.

For data processed by Google in connection with advertising, exercise your rights directly with Google using the links above, or reset or delete your advertising ID as described there. If you are in the EEA or the UK you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the Last updated date at the top of this page changes with it. Material changes — a new SDK, a new data category, a change to the consent mechanism — will be described in the app's release notes as well as here.

Accuracy

Every figure the app produces is an estimate from the numbers you entered. It does not account for site conditions, local code, load or span. Verify structural, electrical and gas work against your local code and a qualified professional before you build.
