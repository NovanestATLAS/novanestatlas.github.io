This policy explains what ECL English Trainer does with information on your device. It is written to be read, not to be endured.

The short version. The app works offline. We do not create an account for you, we do not ask for your name or your email, and we never send your answers or your scores anywhere. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising service needs in order to show an ad.

1. Information we collect

We, the developer, collect nothing. There is no registration, no login, no server belonging to us, and no analytics tool inside the app. We have no way of knowing who you are or how you use the app.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app useful, the following is saved locally, in the app's own storage, and never transmitted:

  • your scores, your daily goal and your streak of consecutive days;
  • the questions you answered incorrectly, so they can be brought back for review;
  • questions you marked as favourites;
  • your settings: voice, speech rate, theme, text size.

This information stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data from Android Settings, erases all of it permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

3. Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google, acting as an independent data controller. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier;
  • technical information about your device, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. Google's practices are described in its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy, and in the Google Advertising Policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can limit this. On Android, open Settings › Privacy › Ads and either delete your advertising ID or turn on Opt out of Ads Personalisation. Ads will still appear, but they will no longer be personalised.

4. Speech synthesis

The read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your Android device. The text is spoken locally. Depending on the voice you have chosen in Android, your device may use a network voice supplied by its manufacturer or by Google; in that case the sentence being read is processed by that engine under its own terms. Choosing a voice whose name ends in local keeps everything offline.

5. Permissions

The app requests internet access. It is used for one purpose only: loading advertisements. All learning content is embedded in the app and needs no connection.

6. Children

This app is designed for adults preparing a professional language examination. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. To erase everything the app has stored, use Settings › My data › Erase everything inside the app, or uninstall it. For the data held by Google in connection with advertising, exercise your rights directly with Google using the links in section 3.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the date at the top will be updated.

Advertising, the advertising ID, and your choices

ECL & ALCPT English Trainer (com.novanest.ecltrainer) uses Google AdMob for advertising. This section is the disclosure Google requires of every publisher that uses its advertising SDKs, and it matches what is declared in the Play Console Data safety form for this app.

What is collected, and by whom

We do not collect, store or transmit personal data ourselves. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app does collect data in order to serve and measure ads. It reads the Android advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned by your device — and sends it with each ad request, together with coarse technical information such as device model, operating system version, language, and the IP address that every network request necessarily carries.

That identifier is used to select ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance, and detect fraudulent activity. It is sent to Google, which may make it available to its advertising partners. We receive only aggregate reporting — how many ads were shown and what they earned. Those reports contain nothing that identifies any individual.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your controls

  • Reset or delete the advertising ID. On most Android devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving it at all.
  • Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. Where the GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive apply, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform, a Consent Management Platform certified by Google, before any personalised ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy options entry in the app's settings.
  • Non-personalised ads. If you decline consent, ads may still be shown but are not personalised using your advertising ID.
