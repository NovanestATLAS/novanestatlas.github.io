Short version. ECL & ALCPT English Trainer is primarily an offline English-learning application. The developer does not require an account and does not directly collect users' names, email addresses, examination answers, scores, or learning history on a developer-operated server. Learning progress and preferences may be stored locally on the device. The application uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), which may automatically collect and process certain device, advertising, interaction, diagnostic, performance, and approximate-location information for advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and compliance purposes. The application also uses Google Analytics for Firebase, which automatically collects usage statistics (such as app opens, sessions, device type, application version and approximate country) linked to a random app-instance identifier, so that the developer can understand how the application is used and improve it.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Novanest ATLAS handles information in connection with ECL & ALCPT English Trainer.

This policy applies to the Android application identified by the package name com.ecltrainer.english.

The purpose of this policy is to explain what information is stored locally by the application, what information may be collected by third-party services used by the application, why that information is processed, and what choices are available to users.

2. Information the Developer Collects

Novanest ATLAS does not operate a user account system or a developer-operated backend for this application.

The developer does not directly collect or maintain a personal profile containing your name, email address, telephone number, examination answers, examination scores, or learning history.

The developer receives usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase (see section 6). These statistics are linked to a random app-instance identifier and do not include your name, e-mail address, examination answers, or scores.

The application does not require registration or login to use its learning functions.

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Certain information may be stored locally in the application's storage so that learning features and preferences continue to work between sessions.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

  • learning progress and scores;
  • daily learning goals and streak information;
  • questions answered incorrectly for later review;
  • questions marked as favourites;
  • selected voice or text-to-speech settings;
  • speech rate or pronunciation settings;
  • theme and display preferences;
  • text-size or accessibility preferences; and
  • other application preferences necessary for the operation of the app.

This locally stored learning information is not intentionally transmitted to the developer's servers because the application does not use a developer-operated learning-data server.

If you uninstall the application or clear its application data through Android settings, locally stored application data may be deleted. Information stored locally cannot be recovered by the developer because the developer does not maintain a copy of that information.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

ECL & ALCPT English Trainer is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated into the application may automatically collect and share certain information with Google and advertising partners for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, performance measurement, and compliance.

Depending on the applicable Google Mobile Ads SDK functionality and the user's device and consent choices, this information may include:

  • Approximate location: the IP address associated with an internet request may be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • App activity / app interactions: information such as app launches, taps, ad views, and other interactions with the application or advertisements.
  • App information and performance / diagnostics: information related to app and SDK performance, such as app launch time, hang rate, energy usage, diagnostic information, and crash-related information where applicable.
  • Device or other identifiers: identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, or other applicable device/app identifiers used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
  • Technical information: information such as device model, operating system, language, network-related information, and other technical information necessary for advertising and SDK operation.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

The developer does not receive an individual's advertising profile or personal advertising data through ordinary AdMob reporting. The developer generally receives aggregated advertising and performance reporting, such as impressions, clicks, and estimated earnings.

6. Usage Statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

The application uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google, to measure how the application is used: for example how many people open it, how long sessions last, which versions of the application and of Android are in use, and in which countries the application is used. The developer uses these statistics to fix problems and to decide which learning content to improve.

According to Google's published documentation, the Google Analytics for Firebase SDK automatically collects:

  • Device or other identifiers: a random app-instance identifier created when the application is installed and, where available and permitted, the Android Advertising ID.
  • Approximate location: coarse location, such as country or region, derived from the masked IP address of the device.
  • App activity / app interactions: app lifecycle events such as first open, sessions, screen views, engagement time, and application updates.
  • Technical information: device model, operating system version, language, and application version.

The application does not send your examination answers, your scores, your learning history, your name, your e-mail address, or your telephone number to Google Analytics. The developer does not assign a user identifier and has not enabled Google signals.

The developer sees this information mainly as aggregated reports in the Firebase and Google Analytics consoles. It is linked to the random app-instance identifier, not to your identity. Google states that the data collected by the SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

Where personalized advertising requires consent, the application uses Google's consent mechanism where applicable. If a user does not provide consent for personalized advertising, advertising may still be delivered in a non-personalized, limited, or otherwise permitted form depending on the applicable regulatory and technical conditions.

8. Advertising ID

Google Mobile Ads may use the Android Advertising ID, a resettable identifier provided by Android, for advertising-related purposes.

The Android Advertising ID is controlled at the device level. Users can reset or delete the advertising ID through Android's privacy and advertising settings, subject to the Android version and device manufacturer.

Removing or resetting the advertising ID may affect the advertising experience but does not necessarily remove all advertising or prevent contextual advertising.

11. Text-to-Speech

The application provides text-to-speech functionality using the text-to-speech engine available on the Android device.

Depending on the selected voice and Android/device configuration, speech synthesis may be performed locally on the device or by a network-based voice service supplied by the device manufacturer or another service provider.

When a network-based voice service is used, the text being converted to speech may be processed by that service according to its own privacy policy and terms.

The developer does not intentionally collect or store the text-to-speech content on a developer-operated server.

12. Internet Access

The application requests internet/network access primarily to support advertising services, usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), and related Google services.

The core English-learning content is designed to remain available offline and does not require the developer's own online account or learning server.

Network access may also be required when a selected Android text-to-speech voice or other device service operates through the internet.

13. Security and Encryption

The developer does not maintain a database containing users' learning profiles or personal information for this application.

Information transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit using TLS according to Google's published Mobile Ads data disclosure information.

Information transmitted by Google Analytics for Firebase is also encrypted in transit using TLS, according to Google's published data disclosure information.

No security method can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their Android device and operating system appropriately secured and updated.

14. Data Retention

Learning information and preferences stored by the application locally remain on the device until they are deleted by the application, the user, Android system data controls, or by uninstalling/clearing the application's data.

The developer does not maintain a separate copy of this local learning information.

Data processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own retention practices, policies, and applicable legal requirements. The developer does not control Google's independent retention periods.

Usage statistics collected by Google Analytics for Firebase are kept by Google for the data-retention period set in the developer's Google Analytics settings (2 months), after which the event-level data is deleted automatically. Aggregated reports, which no longer identify an app instance, may be kept longer.

15. Deletion and User Requests

The application does not require users to create an account. Consequently, there is no developer-operated account database that a user needs to request to have deleted.

To remove information stored locally by the application, the user may use any available in-app data-reset option or clear the application's data through Android settings. Uninstalling the application also removes the application's local data according to Android's normal application data handling.

Usage statistics collected by Google Analytics for Firebase are not linked to an account. Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application resets the app-instance identifier, and the related event data is deleted automatically at the end of the retention period described in section 14.

For information processed by Google in connection with advertising, users may exercise applicable privacy rights through Google's privacy tools, account controls, device advertising controls, or other mechanisms provided by Google.

16. Children's Privacy

ECL & ALCPT English Trainer is an educational application designed for users preparing for English-language examinations such as ECL and ALCPT. It is not designed or marketed primarily for children under 13.

The application does not knowingly request children's names, email addresses, telephone numbers, or other directly identifying information.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the developer directly, please contact us at quizzy.quest.planet@gmail.com so that the matter can be reviewed and appropriate action can be taken.
