What it is

The naturalisation civics test is spoken aloud, not written down. This app lets you study it the same way an officer will ask it — by ear, at your own pace, with nothing to read and nothing to type.

Your device reads each question aloud, waits while you answer out loud, then reads the accepted answer and a short explanation of why it matters. A commute, a walk, a shift in the kitchen or the school run becomes study time you would otherwise have lost.

Which test applies to you

USCIS selects your version by the date you filed Form N-400, not by your interview date. Both banks are included, and you switch between them in the app:

Filed on or after 20 October 2025 2025 civics test — 128 questions. 20 asked, 12 correct to pass.
Filed before 20 October 2025 2008 civics test — 100 questions. 10 asked, 6 correct to pass.

Check your N-400 receipt notice if you are unsure. Confirm the current requirements at uscis.gov before your appointment.

How you study

  • Hands-free mode. Put the phone in your pocket. It reads a question, pauses while you answer aloud, then reads the answer back. The pause is adjustable from 2 to 15 seconds.
  • Mock test. Real conditions — the officer stops as soon as the result is decided, and so does this. Includes the 65/20 short test for applicants aged 65 or over who have held a green card for 20 years.
  • Quiz. Multiple choice, for when you want to move quickly.
  • Listen & write. Type the sentence you hear, for the writing portion of the English test.
  • Weak spots. Drills only the questions you keep missing.
  • Spaced repetition. Questions you miss come back the next session and lead it. New material is introduced at a steady daily pace rather than all at once, so review always has room.

Answers that change

Some answers depend on who currently holds an office, and some depend on the state you live in — your senators, your representative, your governor, your state capital. The app marks these and tells you to confirm the current answer from the official source before your appointment. Names of national officeholders are updated with each release; state-specific answers are yours to fill in and are saved on your device.

Works completely offline

Every question, answer, explanation and translation is stored on your device when you install. Nothing is downloaded and nothing you do is sent anywhere. Put the phone in airplane mode and the app works exactly the same. No account, no sign-up, no email address, no password.

Free, with ads

The app is free and paid for by advertising. A banner appears on some screens and a full-screen ad occasionally between study sessions. Ads never appear while a question is being read, never when the app opens, and never in the middle of a session.
