What it is

CDL Test Prep helps truck and bus drivers pass the commercial driver's license knowledge tests. Every question is written from the CDL manual, and after each answer you see the right answer, a short explanation and the manual section to reread.

What is inside

CDL knowledge testsGeneral Knowledge, Air Brakes, Combination Vehicles, Hazardous Materials, Tank Vehicles, Doubles/Triples, Passenger and School Bus
Practice modeExplanation and manual section right after each answer
Exam modeA full set scored against the 80% passing mark of 49 CFR 383.135, then a review of every missed question
Study guidesHow to study from the manual, and which tests you need for your license class and endorsements
